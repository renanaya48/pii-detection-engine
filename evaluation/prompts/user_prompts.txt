## HOW TO WRITE AN ENTRY
##   * One entry = one prompt (it may span several lines). Entries are separated by a line containing only ---
##   * Wrap every PERSON mention in double square brackets:   Please email [[Dana Cohen]] about the invoice.
##   * Titles stay OUTSIDE the brackets:                      Dr. [[Dana Cohen]]
##   * Optional other PII:  [[EMAIL:a@b.com]]  [[PHONE:+972 3 555 0100]]  [[CREDIT_CARD:4111 1111 1111 1111]]
##   * Prompts with NO names are welcome too (especially ones with capitalised words that are not people).
##   * Write them the way real prompts look, including mixed registers; do NOT adapt them to what you think the engine
##     handles well. Use realistic but FICTIONAL personal details (no real people's private data).
##   * Lines starting with ## are comments and are ignored.
##
